What is a monster? Probably need to do some historical research, but mainly want
to define the modern meaning.

Video games + movies + board/tabletop games.

Roughly, I get the feeling that monster is a very broad definition.
It mostly seems to be defined by a lack: lack of empathy, lack of rights.
Something you can do whatever you want to and not feel bad about it.

Nothing that actually exists fits these definitions.
Monsters are rooted in reality like the danger of bears or whales, but these are
not monsters.
Also we love to categorize other people as monsters but this is dangerous due to
the disregard of human rights.

The obsession with monsters in modern media is worrying.
We still depend on monsters so much in modern movies and games.
It's a very popular fantasy to be a monster killer. How does this reflect on us?

Bad examples:
* Grounded
* Monster Hunter
* Pokemon

Good examples:
* Subnautica
* Bloodborne
